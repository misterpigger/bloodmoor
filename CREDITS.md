## Music
The score is written in the game's code and played on recordings of real instruments (organ, harp, strumstick, recorder, frame drums, tambourine) from the [Versilian Community Sample Library](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL) by Versilian Studios, released under **CC0 1.0**. The short per-note MP3s in `music/` were prepared by the [Conquest](https://github.com/hoffspot/conquest) project.

The Arena, Pit/Mutiny, duel and training-yard/Tavern themes (Build 86) add more VCSL instruments, also **CC0 1.0**, cut to the same format (mono, 32 kHz, 80 kbps, trimmed and levelled):
- `psaltery-*.mp3` and `pluck-*.mp3`: *Psaltery, Bowed and Plucked* (`Chordophones/Zithers`, LongBow and Pluck articulations). VCSL names notes with C3 = MIDI 60, so `BowedPsaltery_C4` is `psaltery-72`.
- `bell-*.mp3`: *Tubular Bells 2* (`Idiophones/Struck Idiophones`, the `_v2_` hits).
- `wardrum.mp3`: *Bass Drum 1* (`Membranophones/Struck Membranophones`, `BDrumNew_hit_v7_rr1`).
- `darbuka-doum.mp3`, `darbuka-tek.mp3`, `darbuka-ka.mp3`: *Darbuka* hits 1, 2 and 4 (`vl2_rr1`).

## Fighters
- Bodies (since Build 54): [MakeHuman](http://www.makehumancommunity.org) base mesh hm08, morph targets and high-poly eyes ([makehumancommunity/makehuman](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman), `makehuman/data`), and the Mixamo-compatible rig, skin weights and UV masks from [MPFB](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2) (`src/mpfb/data`), all released under **CC0 1.0**. Built into `fighters.json` by `tools/mh/build_mh.py` and `tools/build-mh.mjs`; the skin textures (`assets/tex/skin_*.jpg`) are baked procedurally from the mesh by `tools/mh/bake_skin.py`; `assets/tex/eye_d.png` is MakeHuman's `brown_eye.png` (CC0), downscaled.
- Skins (since Build 126, `assets/tex/skins/*.jpg`, 2048 px JPEGs with the colour carried past each UV island's edge): photographed skins from the [MakeHuman asset packs](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks.html), **CC0 1.0**: `fair` and the shared normal map `skin_n` are Mindfront's *Aksel* skin, `brown` Mindfront's *male African middle-aged*, `pale` Margaret Toigo's *light skin male, freckles*, `old` OnlyTheGhosts' *old Eurasian male* (Skins 02 pack); `light`, `light2`, `olive`, `dark` and `dark2` are the MakeHuman system skins *young / middle-aged Caucasian male*, *middle-aged Asian male* and *young / middle-aged African male* (MakeHuman system assets).
- Hair, beards, brows and lashes (since Build 126, `assets/mhparts/*`, fitted to each build by `tools/mh/garb/proxies.py`; their textures greyed so the look's colour tints them): MakeHuman system assets *short02* (`h_crop`), *short04* (`h_short4`), *eyebrow001* (`brow1`) and *eyelashes01* (`lash1`), **CC0 1.0**; culturalibre *hair 02* (`h_cl02`, Hair 01 pack), grinsegold *Beard Sigmund* (`b_sigmund`) and Rehman Polanski *Moustache Viking* (`b_mous`) (Bodyparts 05 pack), **CC0 1.0**; punkduck *alpha7 long* hair (`h_alpha7`, Hair 03 pack, [makehumancommunity.org/node/482](http://www.makehumancommunity.org/node/482)), **CC BY 4.0**, by punkduck. The hair and beard textures were redrawn with finer strands by `tools/mh/garb/hairtex.py` (Build 129).
- The full beard (`bs_full`, Builds 127–129, shelved in Build 130 but kept in the repo) is our own: strand cards grown from each face by `tools/mh/garb/hairgen.py`, with a generated strand texture (`strands_d.webp`).
- Garments (since Build 126, `assets/mhparts/{shirt,cote,doublet,aketon,belt_*}.bin`): our own, modelled from each build's body in Blender (bpy) and draped by cloth simulation (`tools/mh/garb`). Wool cloth: [Poly Haven Caban](https://polyhaven.com/a/caban) (`wool_*.jpg`) and [Wool herringbone](https://polyhaven.com/a/poly_wool_herringbone) (`twill_*.jpg`), **CC0 1.0**, greyed for dyeing.
