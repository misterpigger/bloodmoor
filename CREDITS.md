## Music
The score is written in the game's code and played on recordings of real instruments (organ, harp, strumstick, recorder, frame drums, tambourine) from the [Versilian Community Sample Library](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL) by Versilian Studios, released under **CC0 1.0**. The short per-note MP3s in `music/` were prepared by the [Conquest](https://github.com/hoffspot/conquest) project.

## Fighters
- Bodies (since Build 54): [MakeHuman](http://www.makehumancommunity.org) base mesh hm08, morph targets and high-poly eyes ([makehumancommunity/makehuman](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman), `makehuman/data`), and the Mixamo-compatible rig, skin weights and UV masks from [MPFB](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2) (`src/mpfb/data`), all released under **CC0 1.0**. Built into `fighters.json` by `tools/mh/build_mh.py` and `tools/build-mh.mjs`; the skin textures (`assets/tex/skin_*.jpg`) are baked procedurally from the mesh by `tools/mh/bake_skin.py`; `assets/tex/eye_d.png` is MakeHuman's `brown_eye.png` (CC0), downscaled.
- Earlier bodies (Builds 33–53, still buildable with `tools/build-fighters.mjs`): [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com) — *Universal Base Characters* and *Modular Character Outfits – Fantasy* (Standard editions), released under **CC0 1.0**. Taken from the CC0 redistribution in [OpenAgentsInc/openagents](https://github.com/OpenAgentsInc/openagents/tree/main/assets/verse/characters/quaternius), which keeps the original licence texts. Textures were downscaled for the web.
- Skeleton and idle/walk/run animations: Mixamo X Bot (Adobe Mixamo). The bodies are rebound to this skeleton (joints moved onto each body, rest rotations kept so the animations fit).

## Textures and sky
Photo-scanned surfaces and the daylight sky are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), released under **CC0 1.0**. They were downloaded by `tools/fetch_cc0.py` and resized and repacked for the web (colour, normal, and a packed map with ambient occlusion in red and roughness in green); `assets/cc0.json` lists them.
- Ground gravel: [Rocky Trail](https://polyhaven.com/a/rocky_trail)
- Arena sand: [Gravelly Sand](https://polyhaven.com/a/gravelly_sand)
- Stone walls: [Castle Wall Slates](https://polyhaven.com/a/castle_wall_slates)
- Sky: [Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky)

Gear materials (since Build 58), released under **CC0 1.0**, fetched by `tools/fetch_cc0_2.py` (on the `cc0-fetch` branch) and resized to 512 px and repacked:
- Brushed steel roughness (`steel_r.jpg`): [ambientCG Metal009](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Metal009)
- Riveted mail (`mail_d.jpg`, `mail_orm.jpg` with opacity, roughness and metalness): [ambientCG Chainmail004](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Chainmail004)
- Linen weave (`cloth_d.jpg`, `cloth_n.jpg`): [ambientCG Fabric062](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Fabric062)
- Wood for hafts (`wood2_d.jpg`, `wood2_n.jpg`): [ambientCG Wood049](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Wood049)
- Leather for grips and belts (`leather_d.jpg`, `leather_n.jpg`): [Poly Haven Brown Leather](https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_leather)
