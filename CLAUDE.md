# Bloodmoor: notes for Claude Code

A browser sword-fighting game: battle royale plus Duel, Tournament and Gauntlet modes, with peer-to-peer multiplayer. Hosted on GitHub Pages straight from `main` (no build step), so anything merged to `main` goes live within a minute or two.

## Layout
- `index.html`: the whole game: HTML, CSS and one ES module. Sections are marked with `// ====… section name` banners. Search those rather than reading the file top to bottom (it's ~3,000 dense lines).
- `fighters.json`: the fighter models as glTF JSON with an embedded buffer, rebuilt into a GLB in memory at load; don't fetch it as a data: URL. Since Build 54 these are realistic **MakeHuman** bodies (CC0): four builds `MH_Body_A`–`D` (each with `MH_Eyes_*`) sharing one Mixamo X Bot skeleton, built by `tools/mh/build_mh.py` (base mesh + morph targets + MPFB's Mixamo rig/weights) then `tools/build-mh.mjs` (limbs reposed onto the X Bot's rest pose, joints moved, animations kept). `dressMH()` picks a build from the look (`look.b`, or hashed), tints the baked skin set (`assets/tex/skin_d/n/m.jpg`: colour variation, pores/creases normal map, masks for scalp stubble, beard and cavities) to the skin tone, paints stubble and eyebrows from the masks (`skin_m.png`, alpha = brows), grows short hair, beards and brows as stacked shells cut into strands in the shader (`hairShells`/`hairMat`, from per-vertex scalp/beard/brow weights in the body's colour attribute; `HAIR_STYLE` by `look.h`), layers a tiling micro-normal for close-up skin (`sh_detail`), and grows the cloth from the body (`hsCloth`, with a belt and shader-drawn folds `clothFolds`). Armour uses `ARMOR_OFF_MH`/`ARMOR_FIT_MH` because the joints sit differently. The player asked for these bodies; don't swap them without asking. The old Quaternius build (`tools/build-fighters.mjs`, `dressModel`) still works if fighters.json is rebuilt from it.
- `sfx/`, `music/`: CC0 recordings. Every source is listed in `CREDITS.md`. Only add audio whose licence you've checked, and credit it there.
- `tools/`: headless test harness (Playwright). Not needed to play.

## How the game works (the parts people change most)
- **Sim loop:** `logic(dt)` runs fixed steps of `H = 1/240` through `sim(h)`, then `animate(dt)` per fighter. `frame()` renders and runs audio, HUD and networking.
- **Swords (classic engine):** `class Sword` is a hand point `H` pulled toward `Fighter.handTarget()` by a clamped spring, plus a verlet tip `T` steered by `guideDir()`. Weight: the hand's top acceleration is a muscle force over arm plus weapon mass (`WMASS`, kg at the hand), and committed swings of heavy weapons carry past the aim. Per-weapon handling lives in the `HANDLING` table (one row per weapon, documented above it); `Sword.step` only reads it. Damage multipliers stay on `WEAPONS`. Hits are blade sample points against limb capsules (`CAPS`), resolved in `resolveHit`; between blows `bladeContact` keeps the whole blade out of bodies and pushes the body it presses on (cut, thrust, flat, haft and blunt modes, armour, severing).
- **Rigid-body engine** (menu switch "Rigid body"): Rapier, loaded on demand. Weapons are compound rigid bodies (`wepParts`) driven by a grip force plus wrist torque (`physStep`, tuned via `PT`). Limbs are capsule bodies sprung to the animation. A contact-free "shadow" copy runs the same spring, so only real shoves get drawn (`physParts` and `physVisual`). Damage comes from solver contacts in `physCombat`.
- **Looks:** `look = {o: outfit, h: hair, bd: beard, hc: hair colour, sk: skin}` lives on each fighter, in `PROFILE`, in the join hello and in rosters. Team colours (`pal`) dye the undyed cloth through `tintMaterial`. Armour was shaped for a slimmer body and is widened per bone by `ARMOR_FIT`/`ARMOR_OFF`.
- **Grip and pose:** `gripHand()` puts the hands on the weapon; the guard height is set in `handTarget()`. Check pose changes with screenshots, not just numbers.
- **Modes:** the `// modes` section. The training yard (`yardStart`, `yardHit`, `yardTick`) is the quickest place to feel a weapon change; its readout shows tip speed and every hit. `RUN` holds mode state. `modeKill()` decides round ends and `modeTick()` runs timers. `startCountdown()` runs at every match or round start.
- **Grip:** `gripHand(...,wrap)` lines the hand up with the forearm and lays the haft across the palm (`GRIP_IN`, `GRIP_UP`), since a round grip turns freely in the fist. Don't rotate the hand to follow the edge, which bends the wrist. Turning the hand over is the forearm rolling about its own elbow-to-wrist axis (`forearmRoll`; never its local Y, which on these bodies moves the hand), so the wrist itself never wrings. The wrist joint limit (`WR_MAX`/`WR_MIN`, against `Fighter.foreF`) keeps the blade between 12° and 112° of the forearm in both engines. In the rigid-body engine the wrist torque scales with hand spread (`Sword.lever`).
- **Aiming and view: keep this exactly as Build 33.** The player tried several changes (Builds 34–40) and wants the original. The mouse moves the sword hand in a box; past its edge the overflow turns the body (sideways) or tilts the view (up and down), and the camera is the low over-the-shoulder one (`CAM_TP`). Exceptions: `camClear()` pulls the camera in when another fighter, a body or the pit's wall is between it and you (the player approved this in Build 48); no turning while empty-handed (that fixed drift after a throw), and bare fists reach lower for stooping. Scrolling the wheel puts a human's sword in a thrusting stance (`pointT`), so repeated scrolls poke along the line; scrolled right back (`ctrl.z<.42`) it's straight into guard. A jab is the arm driving out with the weapon: swords no longer slide through the hand (`slide` 0); only a spear's shaft slides through the front hand.
- **How weapons are held:** `TWO_HANDED` (longsword, greatsword, axe, spear) keeps the off hand on the haft, and the end of `handTarget` keeps the aim within its reach (not spears). A spear is held low with the back hand at the waist and the front hand taking the shaft wherever it reaches it, ahead of the back hand; two hands until thrown. One-handed weapons put the off hand up in a guarding fist. `rollLimit()` and a 400°/s roll-rate cap stop the head spinning round the haft in both engines.
- **Throwing:** holding Q winds the weapon back over the shoulder (`throwHold`) and coils the torso; releasing drives the arm through for `THROW_T` (0.2 s) while the shoulders unwind and the weight steps forward, then `throwWeapon()` launches it and the empty hand follows through (`throwF`). A spear flies point-first; other weapons tumble.
- **Grabbing** (unarmed humans): `grabStep`/`grabTry`. A slow open hand on a body part closes on it and pulls a standing fighter off balance (`balV`); keep hauling and they're dragged down while you keep hold (`G.haul`). A held body is dragged by giving the limb speed, so when let go it carries on; or drags a ragdoll particle (smoothly, at most 7 m/s, and the body's weight slows and tethers the dragger: `dragT`). Grabbing a severed arm or leg, or pressing E near one, makes it a club (`debrisWeapon`); grabbing a bridge plank tears it loose. Reaching low sets `stoop`, which crouches the body and goes out in snapshots as `cr`.
- **The Pit map:** `PIT` (shaft radius and depth, `spikes`, `planks`). The player wants a **wooden bridge** across the middle, not Half Sword's iron grate (Builds 43–48 had a grate; it's gone). Each plank is a `PROPS` entry (`kind:'plank'`, `PIT.p0` + k·0.19 m) that breaks under blows or can be wrenched loose by a stooping hand (`grabTry`); `onBridge()` is false over a gap, so whoever stands there goes down the shaft, and in the rigid-body engine each plank has its own collider. A broken plank often leaves its piece lying loose on the deck to be picked up or grabbed (any loose weapon under an open hand is taken). Hits and broken planks wear down `PIT.integ`; six gone in a row, over half gone, or the frame worn out brings the whole bridge down (`bridgeCollapse`, event `B`) as one piece, like Half Sword's grate: beams and remaining boards are grouped and handed to the old grate machinery (`PIT.grate` with `bridge:true`, `GR` resized), so it tips over the sounder rim, slides off, falls and ends up wedged across the shaft leaning on the far wall, where it's a floor (only once at rest) but too low to climb out. Once a body's hips are below the deck the deck no longer holds any part of it (`Ragdoll.under`), which stopped falling bodies hooking on the edge and launching. The old grate code (`GR`, `grateStep`, `grateSlab`) is dormant while `PIT.grate` is unset. Every floor check uses `groundY(x,z,y)`, plus `pitWall()` below the rim. Walking or being shoved off the edge, you drop on your feet for about 0.4 s (`fallT`, gravity to `groundY`) and then tumble (`pitFall()`); straddling the lip you tumble at once. You die only at the bottom, on a stake (`Ragdoll.pin`) or from the landing. Stakes also ring the top of the shaft wall. Bots steer away from the edge.
- **Look (Half Sword-style):** `buildYard()` is The Yard, a walled castle yard with a fenced ring; `buildArena()` is The Arena, the sand colosseum with a crowd (map key `colosseum`, the default for arena modes when coming from the village). Surfaces start as procedural textures (`gravelMaps`, `limestoneMaps`, `paleWoodMaps`) and `loadPhotoTex()` swaps in CC0 photo-scanned sets from `assets/tex` (`PHOTO` lists which material gets which) plus a Poly Haven HDRI sky for daylight. Day light is a hard warm sun with a 4096 shadow map tightened round the player (`shR`/`shMap` in `LIGHTS`), plus GTAO ambient occlusion (sprites and see-through effects are kept out of it, or they draw dark squares) and a teal-orange grade.
- **Stance and hit reactions:** fighters keep a deep, staggered fighting stance (`stanceAmt`, `stillAmt`) and keep their knees bent on the move. The spine-to-head chain is a physical body (`bodyStep`, classic engine; the rigid-body engine has its own limbs): masses on muscle springs (`BODY_K`) follow the animation, blows push the struck part (`bodyHit(bone,dir,dv,drop)`), and `drop` weakens the muscles for a daze. Where a blow lands sets the reaction in `resolveHit`: head snaps, a gut blow folds the body, a leg cut buckles the knee (`pbd.buckle`). Knocked-down fighters throw their arms out (`Ragdoll.reflex`) and get up in stages (`startRise`/`riseStep`: hands and knees, one knee, then a low crouch close to the animated pose; someone flat on their back rolls onto their front first; each stage's targets travel smoothly from where the body was, and the weapon follows the hand throughout, so the hand-over to animation doesn't pop), slower and shakier the more hurt they are, sometimes slumping back. A blow while getting up knocks them back down (`Ragdoll.strike`), and bodies on the ground, dead or alive, can still be struck and cut (`combatStep` includes ragdolls; `Ragdoll.drop` removes a severed part).
- **No bars (as in Half Sword):** blood and stamina aren't shown in any mode; Tab shows the wound diagram. Stamina is felt: blood loss caps it (`35+65*hp/100`) and slows recovery, moving slows it more, tired arms sink the guard (`handTarget`), and the player hears ragged breathing when winded or bleeding (`breathStep`).
- **Healing (as in Half Sword):** out of the fight (no damage for 9 s, not bleeding) flesh wounds close: `heal()` restores blood and limb health slowly; severed or ruined limbs stay that way. Bots with no hands left, or bleeding out, run from their attacker.
- **Injuries (no damage bar):** `hp` is blood and vitality; wounds bleed through `addBleed(rate,tau)`, each clotting on its own (the neck barely clots; a stump pumps for a long time). Limbs keep their own health (`LIMB_HP`, `limbF`): a hurt leg limps and can't run, a ruined one gives way (`injureLimb`); a hurt sword arm swings weaker and a ruined arm hangs useless, so the weapon goes to the other hand. A cut throat or a bad gut wound sends the free hand to it (`startClutch`/`clutchAt`). These reach clients as the `L` and `q` events.
- **Blood on bodies:** every cut or stab (and every stump) is a blood source (`B.bloodSrc`); `bodyBlood(f)` sends the ten biggest to the fighter's shared shader uniforms (`mats.bloodU.uWd`, world position + amount) each frame, and `tintMaterial`'s shader wets the skin round each wound and runs it downward in world space, thin rivulets on skin and a broad soak on cloth. Amounts grow while the fighter bleeds and for a few seconds after death. Spray drops are fine and streak along their flight. A beheaded body stays up for a moment and then folds (`Ragdoll.hold`).
- **Dismemberment:** `cutFace()` caps stumps and severed parts with a flesh cross-section (skin, fat, muscle, bone and marrow, `fleshCanvas`), and spurts pump with a heartbeat along the cut.
- **Small details** (the `// small details` section, Build 57): faces carry three morph targets built from MakeHuman's expression units (`blink`, `jaw`, `pain` in `fighters.json`, made by `tools/mh/build_mh.py`), driven by `fighterDetail()`: blinking, a grimace when hurt or spent, panting, half-shut eyes when dazed, and on the dead sagging eyelids and a slack jaw. The chest heaves with breathing in `animate`. `tintMaterial` also takes per-fighter `uSweat` (skin gloss, shirt soaked down the chest), `uDirt` (sand on knees, back, seat, hands once a body has been down) and per-wound `uWa` (blood browns and goes matte over a minute), and tears the cloth open round each wound. Repeat cuts in one spot widen the gash; bruises come up and darken. Blades drip, dry and pick up scratches and nicks against armour (`Sword.worn`, `uWear`, sent as `w[11]`). Plate sparks and dents (`dentArmor`, event `D`), mail thuds, a heavy head blow can knock a helmet flying (`knockHelmet`/`helmetFly`, event `H`). Dropped weapons clatter and get kicked along by walking feet (`kickLoose`). Thrown weapons quiver where they stick. Steps knock hollow on the bridge, mail jingles and plate clanks; falls and running raise dust; motes drift in daylight. Pools spread under the dead and dying and dry dark; the downed press a hand to their worst wound (`Ragdoll.clutch`) and groan; the fresh dead twitch; the dying gurgle; a dismemberment makes the arena crowd gasp. Everything else here is cosmetic and worked out on each machine from what snapshots already carry.
- **Half Sword look (Build 58):** AgX tone mapping. Steel, mail, linen, leather and wood use CC0 photo-scanned maps (`GEAR`, `steelDetail`, `mailDetail`; steel and mail are mapped in each piece's own frame in the shader because primitives have poor UVs). On the MakeHuman bodies, armour is grown from the body itself (`fitShell`/`fitPieces`/`fitArmor`): the region's skin pushed out, smoothed into a shell and bound only to the bones it rides on, so breastplate, fauld, mail hauberk (worn over cloth), vambraces, greaves and the four helmets (skull shells shaped per type, a kettle brim, a nasal, a bascinet with a mail aventail, a drum great helm with a cut sight slit) fit every build. Outfit 2 is a quilted gambeson (`quilt`). Dents work on these skinned pieces through their bone. Blades have a real cross-section (bevelled edges, fuller, diamond near the point) and wheel pommels. Don't move the chest bones for breathing: it shifted the shoulders, arms and camera, and the player felt it in the weapon handling (Build 57).
- **Build 59:** the arena crowd is seated people (`crowdFigure`/`buildCrowd`: instanced, own clothes, skin and hair or hood per person, arms up when they cheer), on plank benches; the arena stone uses the photo wall texture. Loot armour on the ground is the same body-grown pieces laid down (`fittedPreview`). Bearded axe (head tapers to a thin edge) and flanged mace. Fighters turn their heads toward the nearest opponent (head only for the player, so the camera doesn't move). Armed fighters' legs blend toward a fencer's guard and a lower guarded gait (`GUARD`, leg bones only, sampled by hand after the mixer), from Quaternius clips retargeted into `fighters.json` by `tools/anim-retarget.mjs` (`tools/anim/retarget.js` carries world-space rotation changes from the source T-pose to our bind pose). Keep extra clips to the legs: anything that moves the chest moves the sword hand and camera.
- **Spectating (Build 60):** slain in multiplayer (host or friend, any mode), after 2.6 s the camera follows the fighters still standing, your killer first (`SPEC`, `specStart`/`specNext`/`specTick`; click or ←/→ to switch, Esc for the end screen); it stops when only one is left or the run ends (`END`). Single player gets a Spectate button on the end screen. Purely local: it only reads the fighters the snapshot already provides.
- **Physical gait (Build 60, off since Build 61):** the player found it weird and too fast and prefers the walk cycle, so it's off by default (`GAIT_ON`, `?physgait` to try it); don't turn it back on without asking. When on, `Fighter.gaitStep` replaces the looped walk cycle's footwork for living, grounded fighters: each foot steps to where it must land to keep the body's weight over the feet (body position + velocity × time to landing + half a stride, pushed further by `bal` from blows), one foot swinging at a time with a little double support at walking pace, re-aiming during the swing; hips drop just enough for the legs to reach (capped 9 cm), rise over the planted leg and sway onto it. Walking, running, turning on the spot and recovery steps after a shove all come from that rule. The clips still drive the upper body. Measured: planted feet slip about 1 mm per frame; sword hand lag and jerk while walking match the clip gait.
- **Stumbles (Build 62, removed from the menu in Build 63):** the player tried the hybrid and wants the original walk; `HYBRID` is hard-off and the code is dormant. Don't bring it back without asking.
- **Build 63:** no footprints or drag marks (the player found them unnecessary). The get-up roll turns the whole body over together, hips and legs leading with a knee drawn across, so the trunk no longer corkscrews against the legs. `frame()` caps how far looking down tips the guard (`pu`), and `handTarget` leads by 0.062 s of the body's velocity, so running (and running while looking down) no longer leaves the arms trailing behind the body; standing aim is unchanged. Character options: Build (`look.b`: Lean/Athletic/Strong/Heavy = MakeHuman bodies D/A/C/B), Garb (`o`: bare, shirt, tunic 3, jerkin 4, gambeson 2), Head (`hd`: coif, hood, hidden under helmets), Feet (`ft`: shoes, boots), Beard (`bd`: 0 stubble, 1 beard, 2 clean, 3 full, with longer shells), Marks (`mk`: scar, war paint, drawn in the skin shader round `uEyeB`). New clothes are body-grown shells (`fitShell`); `cleanLook`/`randomLook` carry the new keys.
- **Fight feel (Build 64):** bots keep measure: they wait just out of reach (`outD`, further when hurt or blown), circle with changing pace (`A.amp`), step in to strike and step back out after (`A.backT`, also after being hit), and feint about a third of the time scaled by skill (`A.feint`: show one line then switch to another, or break off and step back). Armour by type in `resolveHit`: plate cuts the edge's penetration to 30% (more glances), mail to 55% but lets points through better (×1.3), blunt goes through plate (×1.15); a glancing blade bounces back off (classic engine). Measured over six bot duels: fights 28 s vs 25 s, still decisive, and they stand further apart between exchanges (3.2 m vs 2.4 m mean).
- **Polish (Build 65):** the menu and fighter card fit a 768-high screen (the card scrolls, the preview shrinks on short screens, the five Garb buttons fit); drag the preview to turn your fighter. The shirt takes the Coat colour (Coat and Trim showed nowhere on the default shirt). The preview uses the game's AgX tone curve. The map you pick is remembered (`bloodmoor-map`). Keys and mouse buttons are released on window blur (Alt-Tab), M toggles music anywhere with a small `toast()` (the body-only `note()` swallowed it), no crosshair dot in arena modes, the wound view says Steady/Shaken/Bleeding/Weak/Fading instead of a number, the crowd's clothes are muted. Multiplayer: a friend's HUD follows the host's mode (`clientStart` sets `body.sim`; it used to follow the friend's own menu choice), friends don't get Restart in the pause menu, and the pause text says the fight goes on. Menu text no longer mentions sliding or glowing loot (both gone).
- **Feel and sound (Build 66):** the classic engine's sword hand rides with the body: `Sword.step` adds the owner's measured velocity to the grip's damping and its acceleration as a feed-forward (`_hbv`/`_hba`), so a shove, stagger, stop or collision no longer leaves the hand trailing and then springing back (the old `handTarget` speed lead `vel*.062` is kept only for fists and the rigid-body grip). A resting blade on a body (`resolveHit` below 2.2 m/s) only checks the push into the body instead of braking the hand every step, which froze weapons against people. Measured over bot duels: hand error p50 6.3→2.1 cm, p99 78→48 cm, steps over 30 cm 7.0%→2.8%; running hand lag ~30→~4 cm; stop and shove peaks 21–42→8–12 cm; standing aim unchanged. `bodyPress()` makes downed bodies solid to standing fighters' feet and to each other (wading through corpses 25→0 frames in a test match). Friends' screens draw everyone but themselves from a short snapshot buffer (`NET.buf`, host time `ht`, render clock `NET.rt` about two snapshot gaps behind, events queued to match in `NET.evq`): foe blade jerk p95 245→120 at ±20 ms jitter. Sound: the "helicopter" was the ring of fire: a 0.53 s clip looped (1.9 Hz pulse) over a noise synth chopped by a 13 Hz square wave; both gone. Ambience is now recorded CC0 loops (`AMB`: `amb-fire` for the ring and for torches/braziers by distance in `ambTick`, `amb-crowd` for the Arena murmur and for cheers and gasps via `crowdBurst`, `amb-crickets` in the village), wind is a 12 s brown-noise loop, and the blade swoosh only sounds on real swings (tip over 6.5 m/s). The arena crowd is cheap: ~170 triangles a person, Lambert, no shadows, ten frustum-culled sectors, no head-tracking (frame triangles 2.2M→1.0M).
- **Build 67:** a fighter slain while down or getting up goes limp (`die()` clears `ragdoll.rise`, tone and reflex; before, the dead body finished standing up). The weapon turns with the body in both engines: `Sword.step` rotates hand, head, their momentum, edge and axis about the owner by each step's yaw change (and adds the spin to `tipVel`, so a turning cut still hits hard); `physStep` rotates the rigid body's pose and velocities the same way. A 360° turn in 0.25 s used to leave the hand behind the body for 16 frames; now it stays in front. The Pit's ring fence is solid and the edge of the fight (`addBox` 2.2 m high, too high to vault), with a fenced chute from the gate to the gap in it for the gauntlet; braziers, barrels, crates and hay moved inside it.
- **Combat (Build 68):** *Neck:* on the MakeHuman rig the Head bone sits only 2.7 cm above the Neck bone, so the head and chest capsules swallowed every neck blow (0 neck hits in 68 bot hits, no beheadings). `resolveHit` now counts a head or chest blow as a neck blow when it lands level with the neck, between the Neck bone and a quarter of the way up the head (within 0.2 m: the blade is caught at the capsule's edge): about 6% of hits, and beheadings happen again (sever chance from 0.35 to 0.5). *Organs:* `organAt` works out where a blow went in, in the struck body's frame, and `organHurt` applies it: heart (a thrust left of the breastbone: heavy bleed, down within ~1.5 s, dead in seconds), lung (less wind and slower recovery via `lung`, coughing blood), belly opened by a cut (`gut=1`: no running, clutching, heavy bleed), gut and kidney stabs (bleeds), spine (a heavy blow or thrust to the back of the chest or neck: knocked down 3.5 s, legs weakened), face cut (×1.25 and blood in the eyes, `blindT`: a dark red overlay for a player, wandering aim for a bot; event `bl` to friends), eye (a real thrust: ×1.4, blind 6 s, dazed). Notes for these pass `SIM_NOTE`. *Spear:* the back hand now sits at the hip on its own side (`handTarget` spear branch, at least 0.15 m out from the centreline), so the shaft runs past the body and its butt out behind the hip: spear in own torso went from 97% of frames to ~5%. *Kicks:* a kick staggers and pushes (~0.7 m standing, was ~2 m) and knocks down only when something helps it: a running kick (`kickRun`), kicked from the side or behind (legs trip), already leaning that way, mid-swing, spent, hurt legs. Measured: face to face 1/12 down (was 3/12), running 7/12, from behind 10/12.
- **Village (Build 69):** *The village burns:* up to ten thatched houses in the inner rows (`HOUSES`, built into their own groups via `GEO_TARGET`/`batchGroup` instead of the shared batches) catch fire when the ring of fire comes within 3 m of them, plus one house well inside the ring after 70 s (a spark). - **The Longship, now Mutiny (Build 71, map key `ship`, `buildShip`, `SHIP`, `noRing:true`):** a longship on a grey sea: a deck (`shipIn`/`shipHalfW`; sizes in Build 72 below), a mast with a striped sail, shields and oars along the sides, a dragon-headed prow. `groundY` is the deck inside the hull and the sea bed (-3) outside it, so whoever leaves the deck falls; in the water `shipStep` ends them (`went over the side`, `thrown overboard`, `splash`). Classic wheel thrust from 2.2 m: stuck 6/6. *Tavern weapons in the rigid-body engine* now use their `HANDLING` rows (grip, arm and wrist scaled against the longsword's, `kI`/`aI`/`wI`/`dI` in `physStep`); before only the classic engine read them. *Kick:* where it lands and how he's standing count: a kick to the legs (`lowK`), one off his centre (`offC`), a man moving or in the air all add to the lean; the balance decides. *Own head:* the hand is kept 7 cm (was 12) clear of its own head capsule, which was dragging the hand as it passed the face.
- **Breakable things and improvised weapons:** barrels, crates, fence bays (each stretch of rail between two posts) and bridge planks are `PROPS` with health; in the rigid-body engine each has its own static collider (`phys`), removed when it breaks; `propHitTest` takes weapon blows off them and `propBreak` leaves staves, boards or a plank as pick-up weapons (the `improv` entries in `WEAPONS`, along with severed limbs). A beat on a held spear's shaft knocks its point well off line in both engines (`clash`/`clashPhys`, spear `knockT` weakens the wrist).
- **Smoothness:** the sim only advances in whole 1/240 s steps, so never freeze or crawl it for effect: `hitstop()` eases to half speed. Don't change the camera without asking. `beginRecover()` eases a fighter from ragdoll pose back into animation. Feet plant and step when standing, turning, side-stepping or backing off (`plant`, `sideW`; walking forward uses the clips). Walk and run share one stride (measured clip speeds: walk 1.51 m/s, run 3.62 m/s). On clients, `snapAt()` starts each snapshot blend from what's on screen, and late packets extrapolate briefly.
- **Multiplayer:** the `// multiplayer` section. The host's browser runs the whole sim. Friends (`NET.mode==='client'`) send input about 30 times a second (`clientSendInput`) and render 20 Hz snapshots (`netSnap` → `clientFrame`). Effects and sounds are replayed from `netEv` events. Anything that changes the world on the host must reach clients through the snapshot or an event. Clients never run `Fighter.step`.

- **Simulator feel, not arcade:** in arena modes (`body.sim`) there's no minimap, kill feed, swing meter, score, hit marker, loot glow or freeze-frame; `note()` only shows what your body tells you (`SIM_NOTE`), and Tab shows the wound diagram. Blood loss shows as a darkening red edge and draining colour (`uHurt`/`uDesat`). Walking is 2.35 m/s and running 4.3, slowed by armour and wounds, with slow acceleration; there's no power-slide. Fighters who collide share momentum and knock each other off balance.
- **Gauntlet:** foes come in one by one through each map's `entry` (the gap in the Yard's fence, the Pit's gate, the Arena's far side) and walk out to fight (`RUN.queue`).
