Bloodmoor

A physics sword battle royale with a closing ring of fire. Your mouse is your sword arm: the faster you swing, the deeper it cuts. Put your blade in the way to parry, and take limbs when you can.

Mouse
Move your sword hand. Push past the side (without LMB) to turn. Swinging never moves the view.
LMB
Hold while you swing for full power. Big, fast mouse sweeps hit hardest.
RMB
Hold to look around freely
Wheel
Swords and spears: scroll to thrust; the point stays on line while you keep scrolling. Axes and maces: scroll changes your reach.
WASD
Move · Shift sprint · C crouch · Space jump and vault
F Q
Kick · hold Q to wind up over the shoulder, let go to throw from that hand
V M Tab
Third or first person · music on/off · hold to look over your wounds
Esc
Pause
E
Take weapons and armor, or strip them off the dead
LMBRMB
Unarmed: hold to take control of that fist and swing it with the mouse. Wheel jabs.
G
Tap to switch between fists and open hands. With open hands, a hand you hold out grabs whatever it touches: a collar, an arm, a body on the floor. Haul them off their feet, drag them, throw them. Knocked down or held? Rock A and D in turn to struggle free and get up sooner.

There are no bars: hold Tab to look over your wounds. You hear yourself breathe hard when you're winded or losing blood; tired arms drop the guard and swing weaker. Out of the fight, flesh wounds close; what's cut off stays off. Wild swinging burns your wind, turns your blade flat and glances off armor. Wind up, keep the edge true, and parry to leave them open.

Needs a keyboard and mouse. In battle royale the ring of fire closes in, so stay inside it, and look for better weapons and armour lying about or on the dead.
